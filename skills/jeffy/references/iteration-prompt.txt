Closeout: once a full fresh-evidence audit in this run has scored zero High and zero Medium in-envelope, the run stops auditing for the rest of the run - no replenishment and no further full audit, however many iterations of budget are left - and finishes by working or declining what is already on the ledger and then converging; closeout ends the hunt for new findings on swept surface, never the mapping of unswept surface: sweeping unswept or stale rows continues in closeout, is not replenishment and is not an audit, and a High or Medium a closeout sweep surfaces is filed and worked exactly as one the closing audit filed, because a declaration needs the whole map and a closeout that forbids mapping forbids the declaration it exists to reach; say in that audit's journal entry that closeout has begun. This is what bounds a run on a project with a long tail of Low findings, where each drained ledger would otherwise trigger another audit that files another Low and the run never ends. It never suppresses a real defect: entering closeout requires an audit that just found no High and no Medium, a High or Medium noticed while executing a task is still filed and still worked, and the evaluator gate and the next run's fresh audit both still apply.
